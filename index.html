About BrandLens

Brand rules usually live in scattered documents and in people’s heads. Posts then go out with outdated facts, unsupported claims or the wrong wording.

BrandLens keeps approved knowledge in one place and checks drafts against it before they are published. When it cannot verify something, it says Needs verification instead of guessing. An AI check is never an approval: only the company decides.
